    How TD Mnemonics are Created

    This document organizes TD mnemonics both alphabetically and by usage for clarity.

    Alphabetical Reference

    • ARM: Adjustable Rate Mortgage
    • AU: Australia
    • auto: Automobiles
    • balfed: Balance sheet
    • balrbf: Reserve balance
    • bbb: Corporate bond yield
    • bcr: Business core deposits
    • bfix: Fixed-term business deposits
    • bus: Business loans
    • CA: Canada
    • cci: Consumer Confidence Index
    • cni: C and I (Commercial & Industrial)
    • C: Consumption
    • cpicor: Core CPI
    • cpi: CPI deflator
    • cprof: Corporate profits
    • cre: Commercial real estate price index (CREUS = US, CRECA = Canada)
    • crd: Credit / Credit card loans
    • cur: Currency
    • DA: Germany
    • DE: Denmark
    • dp: Bank deposits
    • DQ: Delinquency rate
    • dy: Charge off rate
    • E: Equipment
    • EA: European Area
    • effr: Effective funds rate
    • eqp: Equity prices
    • ER: Employment rate
    • Fai: Foreign Activity Index
    • FN: Non-residential investment
    • FNIEUS: Non-residential investment in intellectual property and equipment
    • FR: Residential Investment
    • FRO: Residential investment - Other structures
    • FRM: Residential investment - Multi-unit
    • FRS: Residential investment - Structures
    • fx: Bilateral exchange rate
    • g: Government expenditures
    • GDP: Gross domestic product
    • hp: Average home price (e.g. HPONCA = Ontario, Canada)
    • HP1: Single family house
    • HP1MEDUS: Price of single family house
    • H: Real variables
    • hel: HELOC (Home Equity Line of Credit)
    • hh: Households
    • HSL: Housing sales
    • HST: Housing starts
    • jmb: Jumbo loans
    • ldrat: Loan/Deposit Ratio
    • lf: Labour force
    • ln: Loan balance / Chartered bank loans
    • lne: Economy-wide loans
    • lgus: Large time commercial bank deposits
    • M: Imports
    • m1: Money supply (M1)
    • MED: Median? (Used in HP1MEDUS)
    • mmf: Money market funds
    • mtg: Mortgage / Residential mortgage
    • mftot: Total mutual funds
    • nermusda: Nominal effective real exchange rate
    • neer: Nominal effective exchange rate
    • net: Net? (prefix in NX = Net Exports)
    • nlg: Small time commercial deposits
    • nmmf: Non-money market funds
    • nrstus: Retail Sales Total
    • nx: Net exports
    • P: Price (prefix) / Intellectual (middle)
    • pce: PCE deflator
    • pgdp: GDP deflator
    • pli: Personal line of credit
    • plp: Loan plans
    • pcr: Core personal deposits
    • per: Personal deposits
    • pop: Population
    • pr: Participation rate
    • prime: Prime rate (e.g. PRIMEUS)
    • reer: Real effective exchange rate
    • reserves: Balance payments
    • rfx: Real bilateral exchange rate
    • S: Structures
    • sec: Secured loans
    • thh: Total household loans
    • tlvarus: Light weight vehicle sales (Autos + Trucks)
    • tot: Total (e.g. LNETOTCA = total loans in Canada)
    • UK: United Kingdom
    • ur: Unemployment rate (e.g. URONCA = Ontario, Canada)
    • US: United States
    • us: US bond yields (e.g. US10Y)
    • uvpi: Used Vehicles Price Index
    • v: Inventories
    • vixm: VIX index
    • wti: Oil (West Texas Intermediate)
    • ypd: Personal disposable income
    • ypdhus: Real disposable income in US
    • ypo: Nominal personal outlays
    • ypsv: Nominal personal savings

    By Usage

    Core Macroeconomic Aggregates

    • GDP: Gross Domestic Product
    • C: Consumption
    • I: Investment (FN = Non-residential, FR = Residential)
    • G: Government expenditures
    • NX: Net exports (X = exports, M = imports)
    • Pop: Population
    • UR: Unemployment rate
    • PR: Participation rate
    • ER: Employment rate
    • LF: Labour force

    Prices & Deflators

    • CPI: CPI deflator
    • CPICOR: Core CPI
    • PCE: PCE deflator
    • PGDP: GDP deflator
    • Prime: Prime rate
    • EFFR: Effective federal funds rate
    • BBB: Corporate bond yield
    • CRE: Commercial real estate price index
    • EQP: Equity prices
    • WTI: Oil price (West Texas Intermediate)
    • UVPI: Used Vehicles Price Index
    • VIXM: VIX index

    Housing & Real Estate

    • HP: Average home price
    • HP1: Single-family house
    • HP1MEDUS: Price of single-family house (US)
    • HSL: Housing sales
    • HST: Housing starts
    • FR: Residential investment
    • FRM: Multi-unit
    • FRS: Structures
    • FRO: Other structures
    • MTG: Mortgage
    • JMB: Jumbo loans
    • HEL: Home equity line of credit

    Banking, Credit & Loans

    • LN: Chartered bank loans
    • LNE: Economy-wide loans
    • THH: Total household loans
    • BUS: Business loans
    • BST: Short-term business credit
    • PLI: Personal line of credit
    • PLP: Loan plans
    • SEC: Secured loans
    • USEC: Unsecured loans
    • CRD: Credit / Credit card loans
    • LDRAT: Loan/Deposit ratio
    • DP: Deposits
    • PER: Personal
    • PCR: Core personal
    • PFIX: Fixed-term personal
    • BCR: Core business
    • BFIX: Fixed-term business
    • CUR: Currency

    Financial Accounts & Other

    • M1: Money supply
    • MMF: Money market funds
    • NMMF: Non-money market funds
    • MFTOT: Total mutual funds
    • BALFED: Balance sheet
    • BALRBF: Reserve balance
    • DY: Charge-off rate
    • DQ: Delinquency rate

    International

    • US: United States
    • CA: Canada
    • AU: Australia
    • UK: United Kingdom
    • EA: European Area
    • DE: Denmark
    • DA: Germany
    • FX: Bilateral exchange rate
    • RFX: Real bilateral exchange rate
    • NEER: Nominal effective exchange rate
    • REER: Real effective exchange rate
    • NERMUSDA: Nominal effective real exchange rate
    • FAI: Foreign Activity Index

    Income, Outlays & Savings

    • YPD: Personal disposable income
    • YPDHUS: Real disposable income (US)
    • YPO: Nominal personal outlays
    • YPSV: Nominal personal savings
    • CProf: Corporate profits

    Vehicles & Inventories

    • Auto: Automobiles
    • TLVARUS: Light vehicle sales (autos + trucks)
    • V: Inventories
